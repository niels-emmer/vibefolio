Backup & restore

A backup holds everything you have configured here — the services with their uploaded icons and thumbnails, the site settings, the page text, the credit lines and the email settings — as one gzipped JSON file. The SMTP password is never included.

Pre-restore snapshots

Written to the app's data directory just before every restore, so a restore can be undone by restoring the snapshot. The five most recent are kept.

    Restore from a backup

    Restoring overwrites: for each category you select, the archive becomes the truth, and anything it does not mention is removed.
